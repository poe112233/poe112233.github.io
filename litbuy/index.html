Litbuy

Litbuy is the OE desk for collectors. The focus is on pairs where the box, the grade and the colourway matter as much as the shoe itself.

How OE works with this channel

OE does not publish fixed turnaround or fee figures for a channel, because they depend on the item, the source and the destination. The desk confirms the specifics with you before an order is placed.

  1. You send the item — model, colourway or reference, plus size where relevant.
  2. The desk checks sourcing against its own relationships for this category.
  3. You receive availability, condition notes and any caveats before committing.
  4. Only after you confirm does the order move to fulfilment.

Frequently asked questions

How does Litbuy differ from Oopbuy?

Oopbuy is release-led and size-run focused. Litbuy is collector-led — grading, packaging and scarcity come first.

What does a graded box mean here?

The condition notes cover the box and packaging as well as the pair, so you know what you are receiving before you confirm.

Can you source a specific rare pair?

Send the model, colourway and size. The desk reports what it can find and how the piece is graded.
