How OE works with this channel

OE does not publish fixed turnaround or fee figures for a channel, because they depend on the item, the source and the destination. The desk confirms the specifics with you before an order is placed.

  1. You send the item — model, colourway or reference, plus size where relevant.
  2. The desk checks sourcing against its own relationships for this category.
  3. You receive availability, condition notes and any caveats before committing.
  4. Only after you confirm does the order move to fulfilment.

Frequently asked questions

When should I use Boonbuy instead of a category desk?

When your order spans more than one category. A single-category order is usually handled faster by the specialist desk for that category.

Does Boonbuy have its own sourcing relationships?

It draws on the relationships behind the other OE channels and consolidates the result into one order for you.

Can I still specify each item exactly?

Yes. Send the model or reference for each line, and the desk confirms every item in the basket individually.
