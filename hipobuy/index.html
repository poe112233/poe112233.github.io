How OE works with this channel

OE does not publish fixed turnaround or fee figures for a channel, because they depend on the item, the source and the destination. The desk confirms the specifics with you before an order is placed.

  1. You send the item — model, colourway or reference, plus size where relevant.
  2. The desk checks sourcing against its own relationships for this category.
  3. You receive availability, condition notes and any caveats before committing.
  4. Only after you confirm does the order move to fulfilment.

Frequently asked questions

How is Hipobuy different from Uidbuy?

Uidbuy builds foundations — outerwear, tailoring and basics. Hipobuy leads with statement and runway-adjacent silhouettes, then balances them.

Are the pieces difficult to wear day to day?

The desk deliberately pairs each statement piece with a wearable daily item, so a basket is not left sitting in the wardrobe.

Can I mix Hipobuy and Uidbuy items in one order?

Yes, they are frequently combined. A basket that also spans other categories can route through Boonbuy instead.
